How we met

Our story began when Prakhar stumbled upon a blog I had written and decided to trust his intuition and find a way to connect with me. What started as a simple conversation soon turned into a journey of travel, adventure, shared experiences, and discovering not just the world, but each other too. Along the way, we found balance in the most unexpected way. My curiosity and endless desire to try new things was complemented by Prakhar's patience, perseverance, and his quiet way of giving himself completely to whatever truly mattered. Somewhere between the adventures, laughter, and quiet moments, we found home in each other. And now, with full hearts and a lifetime of memories ahead of us, we're ready to take the next step together.

Events

6th July

Mehendi

A beautiful pre-wedding ceremony where intricate henna designs are applied to the bride's hands and feet. Join us for vibrant celebrations with music, dancing, and joyful moments.

Haldi

A sacred ritual where turmeric paste is applied to the bride and groom for blessings of prosperity and happiness. Get ready for some colorful fun!

Sangeet

An evening filled with music, dance performances, and celebration. Come showcase your moves and join us for an unforgettable night of entertainment!

7th July

Varmala

The exchange of garlands between the bride and groom, symbolizing their acceptance of each other. A moment filled with love, laughter, and friendly competition!

Phere

The sacred wedding ceremony where we take seven rounds around the holy fire, making seven vows to each other for a lifetime of togetherness.

Reception

Join us to celebrate as we begin our new journey together! An evening of dinner, music, and making memories with our loved ones.

Mehndi 1PM - 5PM

Ethnic indian wear
Ladies can wear something bright & vivid and gentlemen can be on their vibrant kurtas with pants or pyjamas along with an optional shawl.

Cocktail Night 7PM - 12AM

Western attire
A semi-formal look that is contemporary & fashionable which is not too prim & proper is the best to pull off this night. You can see some pins for ladies & gents.

Wedding 6PM-12AM

Indo-western
Ladies can flaunt their unconventional sarees, lehengas, anarkalis, dresses and the list goes on whereas gentlemen can wear any kind of suit or just blazer. If you don't like to wear a suit/blazer, you can do a high-neck (band gala) waistcoat & shirt combo.
