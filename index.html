Experience Old Indore

Time needed: Half day

Rajwada Palace

The heart of old Indore — a 200-year-old royal palace that carries the legacy of the Holkar dynasty. Walk through the lanes around it to feel the old city charm.

Krishnapura Chhatri

Beautiful riverside cenotaphs with intricate Maratha architecture. A peaceful spot, especially around sunset.

Lal Bagh Palace

A glimpse into Indore's royal past with grand interiors and European-style architecture.

The Food Trail

Time needed: Evening

Sarafa Bazaar

Jewellery market by day, legendary street-food paradise by night.

Must try:

  • Bhutte ka Kees
  • Garadu
  • Coconut Crush
  • Sabudana Khichdi
  • Joshi ke Dahi Vade
Chappan Dukan

Indore's iconic food street with 56 shops full of snacks, sweets, and cafés.

Must try:

  • Johny Hot Dog
  • Khopra Patties by Vijay Chaat House

Spiritual Escape

Time needed: 1-2 days

Ujjain (55 km)

One of India's oldest cities and home to the sacred Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga.

Explore:

  • Mahakaleshwar Temple
  • Mahakal Lok Corridor
  • Ram Ghat
  • Bhasm Aarti at Dawn
Omkareshwar (80 km)

A peaceful island shaped like Om, around the Narmada river and home to another Jyotirlinga.

Explore:

  • Omkareshwar Temple
  • Narmada ghats
  • Boat ride
  • Evening aarti

A Day Around Nature

Time needed: Half/full day

Patalpani Waterfall

A scenic waterfall surrounded by hills, a quick refreshing escape from the city.

Tincha Falls

Another beautiful monsoon favourite near Indore.

Janapav Hills

A peaceful drive, beautiful views, and the birthplace associated with Parshuram.

The Heritage Road Trip

Time needed: Full day / Overnight

Maheshwar

A quiet riverside town that feels frozen in time.

Explore:

  • Ahilya Fort
  • Narmada ghats
  • Handloom saree workshops
  • Sunset boat ride
Mandu (95 km)

A magical ancient city built among hills and ruins.

Don't miss:

  • Jahaz Mahal
  • Rani Roopmati Pavilion
  • Baz Bahadur Palace
  • Sunset viewpoints

A beautiful mix of architecture, stories, and landscapes.

How do I get there?

By Air

Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport is well connected with direct flights from major cities including:

  • Mumbai (1 hr 20 min)
  • Delhi (1 hr 30 min)
  • Bangalore (2 hr)
  • Pune (1 hr 30 min)
  • Hyderabad (1 hr 40 min)
  • Ahmedabad (1 hr 15 min)

Airport → Venue: 20-30 mins

By Train

Indore Junction is one of Central India's major railway hubs.

Popular routes:

  • Mumbai → Indore (12-15 hrs)
  • Pune → Indore (14-18 hrs)
  • Delhi → Indore (12-14 hrs)
  • Ahmedabad → Indore (8-10 hrs)

Station → Venue: 15-25 mins

By Road

Indore is connected via excellent highways from:

  • Ujjain (1 hr)
  • Dewas (45 mins)
  • Bhopal (4 hrs)
  • Ahmedabad (7 hrs)
